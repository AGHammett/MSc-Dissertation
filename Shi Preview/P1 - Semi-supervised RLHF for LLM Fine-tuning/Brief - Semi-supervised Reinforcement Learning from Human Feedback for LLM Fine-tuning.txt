Semi-supervised Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback for LLM Fine-tuning
Supervisor: Dr. Chengchun Shi c.shi7@lse.ac.uk
We have entered an era of large language models (LLMs), which have rapidly transformed how we
learn, work and communicate with each other. These models, with billions of parameters, can
generate remarkably human-like text. Yet, despite their impressive capabilities, ensuring that LLMs
behave in ways that align with human values – being helpful, honest, and safe – remains one of the
most urgent challenges in generative artificial intelligence.
To address this, researchers commonly apply reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) to
fine-tuning LLMs based on human preferences among multiple responses. However, this approach
depends heavily on expensive human feedback, often resulting in small labeled datasets. This data
scarcity can lead to inefficient training and unpredictable model behavior.
This project studies semi-supervised RLHF — an approach that integrates semi-supervised learning
(SSL) to enhance existing RLHF algorithms by using both labeled and unlabeled data. While LLMs are
typically trained with massive amounts of unlabeled text, to our knowledge, SSL has rarely been
applied to RLHF. Building on recent advances in SSL, this project will leverage the rich information in
unlabeled data to improve LLM’s alignment with human values. The project will be coding-intensive. Its
successful completion is expected to lead to a research paper suitable for submission to a (toptier)
machine learning conference or statistical journal.
